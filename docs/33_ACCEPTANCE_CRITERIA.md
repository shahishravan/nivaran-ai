# Acceptance Criteria

## Recovery Command
- Shows the full recovery chain.
- Starts from a visible customer signal.
- Ends with evidence and a sealed synthetic receipt.

## Decision Lab
- Compares three paths.
- Shows consequences and required steps.
- Requires a human decision before recovery advances.

## Review Inbox
- Supports filtering, searching, manual entry, and CSV demo import.
- Shows urgency, category, status, and evidence.

## Recovery Control
- Displays owner, SLA, customer action, corrective task, evidence, and progress.
- Cannot describe incomplete evidence as verified.

## Evaluation Lab
- Exposes synthetic test slices and guarded escalation.
- Clearly labels metrics as prototype results.

## Agent System
- Shows specialist stages, structured outputs, policies, and approval boundaries.
