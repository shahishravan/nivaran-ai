# Privacy and Data Handling

## Prototype
All customer names, reviews, scores, channels, operational outcomes, and recovery evidence are synthetic. No production customer data is required.

## Production principles
- Collect only data needed for recovery.
- Separate identity from analytical features where possible.
- Mask phone, order, payment, and address details in model context.
- Apply tenant isolation and role-based access.
- Define retention by case type and legal need.
- Support correction, deletion, and export workflows.
- Record access to sensitive evidence.

## Model boundary
Customer data must not be used for training without an explicit lawful basis and clear disclosure.

## Demo language
Claims must say “synthetic prototype,” never imply that displayed names, metrics, or outcomes came from real businesses.
