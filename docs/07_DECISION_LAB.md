# Decision Lab

Decision Lab makes recommendations inspectable by comparing three paths.

## Do nothing
No customer contact, no containment, and public risk continues to compound.

## Reply only
The customer receives acknowledgement, but the operational cause and outcome remain unverified.

## Full recovery
Customer recovery and operational containment run together, with evidence required before closure.

## Integrity
The prototype uses declared synthetic scenario scores. It does not present them as measured production predictions.

## Future validation
Production weights would be calibrated against real, consented recovery outcomes and monitored for segment-level error.
