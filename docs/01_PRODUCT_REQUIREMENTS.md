# Product Requirements

## Product promise
Nivaran converts public customer feedback into a safe decision, an owned recovery workflow, an operational correction, and verifiable outcome evidence.

## Primary user
A local-business owner or operations lead responsible for reviews, customer recovery, and recurring service failures.

## Core jobs
- Rank feedback by customer and reputation risk.
- Explain the evidence behind urgency.
- Compare ignore, reply-only, and full-recovery paths.
- Require human approval before consequential actions.
- Track owner, SLA, corrective task, and outcome evidence.
- Close a case only when recovery evidence is complete.

## Prototype success
The judge can complete the Signal → Decision → Action → Evidence → Recovery journey without external data or private customer information.

## Non-goals
Production authentication, automatic publishing, real refunds, and claims of real-world model accuracy are outside this synthetic prototype.
