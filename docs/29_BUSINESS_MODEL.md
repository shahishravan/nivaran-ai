# Business Model

## Customer
Local and multi-location businesses where public reviews directly affect revenue and operations.

## Value
Reduce missed high-risk complaints, shorten recovery time, connect customer service with root-cause correction, and create an auditable outcome trail.

## Packaging direction
- Starter: review inbox and guided recovery
- Growth: team workflow, SLAs, playbooks, and integrations
- Multi-location: policy governance, benchmarking, and centralized operations

## Revenue
Subscription pricing can scale by location, active recovery cases, or connected channels. High-value onboarding and custom integrations may be service revenue.

## Economic proof
Validate willingness to pay using recovery time, repeat-issue reduction, evidence completion, retention, and owner workload—not synthetic trust scores.
