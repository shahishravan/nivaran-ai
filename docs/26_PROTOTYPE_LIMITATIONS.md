# Prototype Limitations

- Review data, outcomes, scores, and benchmark results are synthetic.
- Agent behavior is deterministic for reliable judging.
- Login is a client-side convenience gate, not production authentication.
- No response is published.
- No refund, order, CRM, or helpdesk action is executed.
- State is not a durable multi-tenant production store.
- Metrics do not prove real-world accuracy or business impact.
- Decision-path values are scenario demonstrations, not calibrated forecasts.
- Integrations and recovery evidence are simulated.
- Production privacy, security, compliance, and monitoring require server-side implementation.

These limitations are intentional and visible. The prototype validates the product workflow and decision boundaries rather than pretending to be production infrastructure.
