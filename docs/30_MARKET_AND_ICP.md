# Market and Ideal Customer Profile

## Initial segment
Independent restaurants, cloud kitchens, salons, clinics, local retail, and service businesses with meaningful public-review volume but limited reputation operations.

## User
Owner, operations manager, customer-experience lead, or location manager.

## Pain
Feedback is scattered, urgent signals are missed, replies are generic, operational causes stay open, and teams cannot prove recovery.

## Buying trigger
A viral complaint, repeated service failure, falling rating, new location, growing review volume, or desire for standardized recovery.

## Expansion
Move from one location to franchise groups and regional operators after proving repeatable recovery workflows.

## Exclusion
Nivaran is not initially positioned for regulated high-stakes decisions without domain-specific policy, security, and compliance validation.
