# Competitor Differentiation

## Common category features
Competitors classify sentiment, cluster themes, prioritize tickets, summarize feedback, draft replies, and produce product reports.

## Nivaran difference
Nivaran continues after analysis:
Signal → Decision → Action → Evidence → Recovery.

## Defensible elements
- Counterfactual decision comparison
- Human authorization boundary
- Customer action plus operational correction
- Evidence-gated closure
- Verifiable recovery receipt
- Visible policies and execution trace
- Evaluation of ambiguity and unsafe automation

## Positioning
Nivaran is not another feedback dashboard or response generator. It is a Review-to-Recovery operating system.

## Integrity
Differentiation claims describe product workflow, not unverified superiority, market share, or production accuracy.
