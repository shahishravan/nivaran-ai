# Responsible AI

## Principles
- **Human authority:** consequential actions require owner approval.
- **Evidence visibility:** urgency and recommendations show supporting signals.
- **Uncertainty:** confidence and guarded escalations remain visible.
- **Outcome orientation:** closure requires customer and operational proof.
- **Data honesty:** benchmark and product metrics are synthetic.
- **Correctability:** owners can reject, edit, or escalate agent proposals.
- **Minimum autonomy:** agents receive only the permissions required for a stage.

## Prohibited claims
The prototype does not claim production accuracy, guaranteed churn reduction, automatic reputation repair, or deployment on real customer data.

## Evaluation
Success includes safe refusal and escalation. A guarded result can be more correct than an overconfident automated action.
