# Product Roadmap

## Buildathon
Deliver the complete synthetic Review-to-Recovery story with visible evidence, decision comparison, approval, evaluation, and receipts.

## Next 30 days
Validate recovery contracts with local businesses, add persistent cases, server-side authentication, and a read-only review import.

## 60–90 days
Introduce policy configuration, controlled model calls, evidence uploads, audit logs, and helpdesk/task integrations.

## 3–6 months
Add multi-location operations, outcome analytics, recovery playbooks, role-based approvals, and calibrated evaluations.

## Later
Build a model-independent Recovery Harness and integration marketplace.

## Release principle
Do not add autonomous writes until policy, approval, idempotency, auditability, and rollback are proven.
