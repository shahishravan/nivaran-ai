# Judge Questions and Answers

## Why is Nivaran different from sentiment analysis?
Sentiment is only the first signal. Nivaran compares actions, assigns recovery ownership, links operational correction, and requires outcome evidence.

## Is the displayed AI real?
The Buildathon prototype uses deterministic synthetic logic so the complete workflow is reproducible. The architecture shows where controlled model calls fit in production.

## Why keep a human in the loop?
Public replies, refunds, customer contact, and operational changes have business consequences. AI proposes; an accountable owner authorizes.

## Are the metrics real?
No. Scores, reviews, names, and outcomes are explicitly labeled synthetic.

## What is the technical moat?
The Recovery Harness: policies, tool permissions, approval state, recovery contracts, evaluation cases, and verified receipts.

## What comes next?
Persistent cases, server-side authentication, read-only imports, controlled model calls, evidence storage, and narrow integrations.
