# Nivaran AI — Demo Script

Target length: **3–4 minutes**.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem and promise

“A negative review is not only a sentiment problem. It can contain customer risk, reputation exposure, and an operational failure. Existing tools summarize the review or draft a response. Nivaran stays until the customer outcome and root cause are verified.”

Show the premium judge login and use:

- Login ID: `judge`
- Password: `nivaran2026`

## 0:20–1:05 — Run Recovery Command

Open **Recovery Command** and click **Run the recovery mission**.

Call out the chain:

1. The public signal is classified with visible evidence.
2. Nivaran selects a safe intervention.
3. The customer action and operational correction run together.
4. A recovery receipt is sealed only after evidence is complete.

## 1:05–1:50 — Compare decisions

Open **Decision Lab**.

Switch between:

- Do nothing
- Reply only
- Full recovery

Explain that Nivaran compares projected consequences instead of hiding the recommendation behind a score. Select **Full recovery** and show the human authorization boundary.

## 1:50–2:35 — Close the loop

Open **Recovery Control**.

Show:

- owner and SLA;
- customer recovery action;
- operational correction;
- resolution evidence.

State clearly: “A reply is not a recovery, and a ticket closure is not proof.”

## 2:35–3:10 — Demonstrate AI-product maturity

Open **Evaluation Lab** and show:

- synthetic benchmark slices;
- sarcasm, mixed intent, and safety ambiguity;
- the guarded human escalation.

Then open **Agent System** to show structured evidence passing between specialist agents.

## 3:10–3:35 — Finish with differentiation

“Competitors organize feedback, prioritize tickets, or recommend product themes. Nivaran is a Review-to-Recovery operating system: Signal → Decision → Action → Evidence → Recovery.”

End on **Recovery Command** with the receipt in the **SEALED** state.

## Recording checks

- Record at 1080p.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%.
- Hide bookmarks and unrelated tabs.
- Use dark mode for the strongest visual contrast.
- Verify that the deployed product and video links open in a private/incognito window.
- Do not claim real-world model accuracy; describe the data and benchmark as synthetic.
